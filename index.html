Set 03 (LEGO Ninjago): Cambridge B2 Part 6 Gapped Text

How to coach Gapped Text logic: Ask Theo to underline the "clue words" right after each gap. For example, right after Gap 1 the text says "Whenever spectators clapped their hands near the table, the dragon roared..."—which proves Gap 1 must introduce the sound-sensor motor (Sentence C).

  1. Gap 1: C — Introduces the programmable sound-sensor motor that explains the roaring/flapping in the next sentence.
  2. Gap 2: A — Explains why the wings were frozen (battery pack vanished), leading into security guards checking the exit.
  3. Gap 3: E — Connects opening the Bluetooth app to following the green radar dot on his screen.
  4. Gap 4: B — Explains how he got the battery back from the toddler without causing a scene, allowing him to reinstall it in two minutes.

(Distractor D is unused because the competition was never cancelled.)

Set 05 (Creator Expert): Coaching Creative Story Logic

Why "Missing Middle" works for non-creative writers: When a student doesn't know what to invent, giving them both the Start and the Ending turns creative writing into a logical puzzle. Look at Bridge 1: the ending says "Thanks to those triangular support pillars..."—so Theo knows he must invent a middle paragraph where the flat bridge collapses and they decide to build triangular pillars!
